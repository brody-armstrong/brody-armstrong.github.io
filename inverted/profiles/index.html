Role map

Roles come from clustering each position group on style alone (volume, pass length and direction, touch share), so a player's role says how he plays, not how well. Click a role to filter the shortlist.

How to read it. g+/96 is American Soccer Analysis goals added above average per 96 minutes, shrunk toward the position-group mean (900-minute prior) so short samples don't top the list. Percentiles compare a player only with his position group. Value is the extra g+ a player produced over the season beyond what a player on his salary typically produces in his position group (robust regression on log salary). It is a shortlist signal, not a verdict: it ignores roster designation, transfer fees, contract length and age curves.

Data: American Soccer Analysis public API (event-derived xG, xPass, g+) and MLSPA salary release of April 2026. Players traded mid-season are shown with their most recent club.

Biggest MLS impact after moving up

Method. For each lower league, MLS per-96 output is modeled as a weighted linear function of the same stat in the lower league plus position-group effects (weights: harmonic mean of the two seasons' minutes). Slopes below 1 build in regression to the mean, so projections are deliberately conservative. USL League One has only 14 direct moves to MLS, so its players are chained through the USL Championship model (68 League One to Championship moves), and the chain was checked against those 14 direct moves. "MLS pct" places the projection within the 2026 MLS distribution for his position group. Comparables are the 2026 MLS players whose actual style profile is closest to his projected one.

Caveats. Only players who earned MLS minutes appear in the fit, so the model describes players clubs chose to promote. Goals added is the least predictable stat here; style and volume numbers carry over far more reliably. Treat the list as a place to start watching video.

How the best squads are built

Share of each position group's minutes (players with 900+ min) going to each role in 2026, top eight teams vs bottom eight.

Method. Team numbers are American Soccer Analysis season totals per game. Driver strength is the correlation with points per game after standardizing within each season. Squad quality is the minutes-weighted g+/96 of everyone who played in a position group; a regression of points per game on the five groups (R² ${R2}) converts each team's shortfall against the top-eight average into points per game. Targets are MLS players on $1M or less with 1,500+ minutes who outperform the team's current level in that group, preferring the role the team lacks, plus USL Championship and League One prospects aged 26 or under from the pathway model.

Caveats. These are associations across seasons, not causes. A missing role can be a coaching choice rather than a hole, so read the role gap as a question to put to the staff. Squad quality credits a player's whole season to his last club when he was traded.
