Timbers2 player passport

One record per player across MLS NEXT Pro and MLS: minutes by tier and season, position KPIs as percentiles against the league, a projection of how each player's game would carry over to MLS, and empty slots for the data only a club holds.

The squad at a glance

2026 MLS NEXT Pro regulars (900+ minutes). Percentiles compare each player with every MLS NEXT Pro player in the same position group with 900+ minutes. The MLS projection is a percentile among MLS players in that group. Select a player to open the passport.

How it is built

  • Data: American Soccer Analysis public API (xG, xA and goals added, g+), MLS NEXT Pro and MLS, 2024–2026. Thanks to ASA for making it public.
  • KPIs: g+ per 96 minutes in total and by action (dribbling, passing, receiving, shooting, interrupting), plus xG and xA per 96. These are stand-ins: a club would swap in KPIs written from its own game model.
  • Flags: green at the 67th percentile or above, red at the 33rd or below, amber between. The bands are a display choice, not a model output, and every flag carries its number.
  • MLS projection: the pathway model from this project, which translates MLS NEXT Pro production to MLS from past move-ups and checks the projection against what happened to earlier players.
  • Club data slots: GPS load, workload ratio, wellness, maturation, development-plan focus areas, video clips and coach evaluations are left empty on purpose. They belong in the same record, keyed to the same player ID, but are not public.
Limits
  • Public event-derived metrics only: no tracking or physical data, so pressing, running and maturation are not measured here.
  • Percentiles do not adjust for age. An 18-year-old is compared with a league of mostly older players, which is exactly why the passport keeps a maturation slot.
  • The MLS projection describes how similar past players' numbers carried over. It is not a prediction of selection, and associations are not causes.
